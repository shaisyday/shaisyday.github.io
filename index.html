Welcome to Evolutionary Gaming

A gaming community focused on helping new players learn basic game mechanics. We focus on DayZ and offer servers with balanced traders, no base raiding, and no PVP. Evolutionary also boasts long-term players who have made the server their home.

We aim to provide a safe, non-toxic space for new and experienced players alike. We encourage exploration, learning, and an engaged community.
